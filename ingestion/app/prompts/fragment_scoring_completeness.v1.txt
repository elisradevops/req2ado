  - completeness_as_requirement: 4 = a complete requirement: a subject, one obligation and a verifiable predicate; 3 = complete but loosely stated; 2 = an obligation that lacks its subject or its verifiable predicate; 1 = a fragment: a value, label, table cell or phrase without a full statement (for example 'Size: 3U 1"', 'Over-current', 'ON/OFF control'); 0 = not a requirement at all: a heading, rationale, an open question or descriptive text. An obligation counts in any language and in whatever form the language states one (a modal verb, a future-tense or imperative form, 'it is required that'), never only the English 'shall', 'should' or 'must'.
