**Design correction (mine, not an owner decision):** the document fallback is NOT the study. Scoring (llm_leveling ~1330) runs before the study is joined (~1406), so a study-based form would either block scoring on the whole study or make prompts depend on thread timing (it breaks L1 determinism and the payload cache). Instead there is one small call at job start, `find_obligation_forms.v1`, over `obligation_sample` (body paragraphs and list items of ≥6 words, ≤4000 chars, in document order).
